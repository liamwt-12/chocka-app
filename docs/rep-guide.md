# Stellar Local: rep guide for connecting a shop

One page for Tarkett reps running the pilot. Allow about 10 minutes per shop.

## Before the visit

1. **Tarkett has already contacted the shop.** Stellar Local never contacts a shop first. The shop should have had Tarkett's message, which names us and carries the short data notice.
2. **You have the shop's link** from Liam: `app.stellarlocal.co.uk/invite/...`. Each link is for one shop only, works once, and expires after 30 days.
3. **Ask the owner to have their Google login to hand:** the Google account they use to manage their shop on Google Maps. This is the step most likely to hold things up.

## At the shop

1. Open the link on the owner's phone or laptop: theirs, not yours.
2. The page shows the shop's name. Tap **Connect my Google profile**.
3. The owner signs in with the Google account that manages the shop's listing, **ticks the box about business listings**, and presses Continue. Google's screen says "Stellar Local".
4. If they manage more than one listing, they choose the right shop.
5. The page says **You're connected**, with the shop's name and address. **That is the whole of it.** Nothing has been changed on the listing.

Everything after that is optional, and the owner can do it then, later, or never:

- **"See what we could improve"** shows suggested changes: description, services, categories, opening hours, replies to good reviews. The owner reads each one and ticks what they want. Opening hours are theirs to type in: we never guess them. Each change is checked on Google before the page says it is done.
- **Two switches**, both off: a short post each week, and replies to good reviews.

No mobile number is needed.

## What to tell the owner

- **It is free to them.** Tarkett pays.
- **They stay the owner of their Google listing.** They can remove us at any time in Google.
- **What happens next:**
  - If they turn posts on: their first three posts wait on their dashboard for them to approve, change or skip. After that, every Friday they get an email with next week's post, and can cancel it. If they don't, it goes out on Monday morning.
  - Replies to 4 and 5 star reviews go out automatically if they turn that on.
  - Replies to 1 to 3 star reviews wait for their approval.
- **Every change we make is logged, and they can undo any of it themselves.** In Settings there is a list of everything we have changed, each with an **Undo this** button. Or they email hello@stellarlocal.co.uk and we reverse it the same working day.
- **What Tarkett sees:** that they take part, and a summary of how their listing is doing. Tarkett does not get their contact details, review text or anything about their customers.

## What not to say

- That Stellar Local adds photos, or asks their customers for reviews. Not yet.
- That we can remove a bad review. Nobody can, except Google.
- Any promised result: more calls, a ranking, a score.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "That Google account does not look after your shop's listing" | Wrong Google account. Press **Try a different Google account** on that page. The link still works. |
| "One box was left unticked" | Press **Try again and tick the box**. On Google's screen, tick the line about business listings. |
| "You stopped at Google's screen" | They pressed Cancel. Explain what the permission is for, then press **Try again**. |
| "Google did not answer" | Wait a minute and press **Try again**. |
| A list of listings, and none is their shop | Press **None of these is my shop** and sign in with the right Google account. |
| "This link has expired" | Ask Liam for a new link. |
| "Waiting on Google" after connecting | The listing isn't verified. The owner must finish Google's verification. We check every day and carry on by ourselves. |
| Anything else | Note the shop and the message, and tell Liam. |

## After the visit

Tell Liam which shops you connected. The pilot admin page shows each one as **live**, or lists it under **Needs help** with the reason and what to do.
