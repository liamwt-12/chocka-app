# Stellar Local: rep guide for connecting a shop

One page for Tarkett reps running the pilot. Allow about 10 minutes per shop.

## Before the visit

1. **Tarkett has already contacted the shop.** Stellar Local never contacts a shop first. The shop should have had Tarkett's message, which names us and carries the short data notice.
2. **You have the shop's link** from Liam: `app.stellarlocal.co.uk/invite/...`. Each link is for one shop only, works once, and expires after 30 days.
3. **Ask the owner to have their Google login to hand:** the Google account they use to manage their shop on Google Maps. This is the step most likely to hold things up.

## At the shop

1. Open the link on the owner's phone or laptop: theirs, not yours.
2. The page shows the shop's name and, where we have one, its Google presence score. Tap **Connect my Google profile**.
3. The owner signs in with the Google account that manages the shop's listing, and agrees to the permissions. Google's screen says "Stellar Local".
4. If they manage more than one listing, they choose the right shop.
5. Stellar Local checks the listing and suggests improvements: description, services, categories, opening hours, and replies to up to 20 unanswered reviews. **The owner chooses what to apply.** Nothing is changed without their tap.
6. They enter a mobile number for weekly text updates. That's it.

## What to tell the owner

- **It is free to them.** Tarkett pays.
- **They stay the owner of their Google listing.** We only ever have manager access, and they can remove it at any time in Google.
- **What happens next:**
  - Every Friday they get an email with next week's post, and can cancel it. If they don't, it goes out on Monday morning.
  - Replies to 4 and 5 star reviews go out automatically if they turn that on.
  - Replies to 1 to 3 star reviews wait for their approval.
- **Every change we make is logged.** If they want anything put back, they email hello@stellarlocal.co.uk and we reverse it the same working day.
- **What Tarkett sees:** that they take part, and a summary of how their listing is doing. Tarkett does not get their contact details, review text or anything about their customers.

## What not to say

- That Stellar Local adds photos, or asks their customers for reviews. Not yet.
- That we can remove a bad review. Nobody can, except Google.
- Any promised result: more calls, a ranking, a score.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "We couldn't find a Google Business Profile" | Wrong Google account. Sign out and try the account that manages the listing. |
| "This link has expired" or "already used" | Ask Liam for a new link. Old links cannot be revived. |
| The listing isn't verified on Google | The owner must finish Google's verification first. Come back after. |
| Anything else | Note the shop and the message, and tell Liam. Don't retry repeatedly. |

## After the visit

Tell Liam which shops you connected. He confirms each one is **live** on the pilot admin page the same day, and lets you know if any need a second look.
