# Invitation wording for Tarkett to send

Stellar Local never contacts a shop first and never sends this message. Tarkett sends it, from Tarkett, to the person at the shop it normally deals with. One message per shop, carrying that shop's own link from `/admin/pilot`.

Words in [square brackets] are for Tarkett to fill in. The data notice is Tarkett's own wording and is not written here.

## The email

**Subject:** Connect [Shop name] to Stellar Local (2 minutes)

Hello [first name],

We've arranged for Stellar Local to look after [Shop name]'s listing on Google: the panel customers see when they search for you on Google or Google Maps. It is free to you. Tarkett pays for it.

**To start, open this link and press the button. It takes about two minutes and works on your phone.**

[the shop's link]

Three things worth knowing before you do:

1. **Use the Google account that looks after your shop's listing.** That is the one you would use to reply to a review or change your opening times. If someone else set the listing up for you, forward this email to them instead.
2. **Google will ask your permission** for Stellar Local to manage your business listing. Tick the box next to it and press Continue. It does not give anyone access to your emails, photos or files.
3. **Nothing changes on your listing when you connect.** You are shown each suggested change and choose which ones you want. You stay the owner, and you can remove Stellar Local at any time.

The link is for [Shop name] only and works for 30 days.

If you get stuck, reply to this email or write to hello@stellarlocal.co.uk with the name of your shop.

[Sender's name]
[Role], Tarkett

[Tarkett's data notice]

## A shorter version, for a text or a message from a rep

Hello [first name], it's [name] from Tarkett. We've arranged for Stellar Local to look after [Shop name]'s Google listing, free to you. Open this on your phone and press the button, it takes two minutes: [the shop's link]. Use the Google account you'd use to reply to a review. Nothing changes unless you choose it.

## If the shop does not respond

The admin page shows whether the link has been opened and how far the shop got. Any follow-up comes from Tarkett, in Tarkett's own words. Stellar Local sends no reminders.

## What not to say

- That it will bring more calls, a higher ranking or any other result.
- That Stellar Local adds photos or asks customers for reviews.
- That a bad review can be removed. Only Google can do that.
- Anything about what it costs Tarkett.
