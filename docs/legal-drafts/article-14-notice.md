# DRAFT, needs solicitor review

> Not sent. Not legal advice. Written 14 November 2026. This is the UK GDPR Article 14 notice owed to the 180 businesses whose records we copied from Tarkett's public store locator in June 2026. Square brackets mark facts to confirm.

## Before anything is sent: read these

1. **This notice is overdue.** Article 14(3) requires it within a reasonable period, and at the latest one month after obtaining the data. The data was obtained in June 2026. `LEGITIMATE_INTERESTS_ASSESSMENT.md` already records this as a live gap.
2. **Send it clean.** It must not contain an invitation, a link to sign up, or any mention of benefits. Adding those turns a legal notice into a marketing email, and under PECR most of this list (sole traders) cannot lawfully be sent one by us. Tarkett makes the introduction, separately.
3. **Who receives it: all 180.** The duty attaches to the record, not to the email address. Treat every record as owed the notice.
4. **Four records have no email** (source refs 29478, 29705, 30089, 29876). Their notice goes by post, to the address in the store-locator copy. Do not rely on the "disproportionate effort" exemption for four letters.
5. **Who sends it.** It comes from us, Useful for Humans Ltd, as controller. Nothing in this repo sends it, and nothing should be built to send it automatically.
6. **What Tarkett sees has changed.** The current in-app notice was written when Tarkett saw only a head count. If the console shows per-retailer health, this notice and the legitimate interests assessment must say so before the console goes live. This draft already does, for participating retailers only.

## The notice (email subject: "How we hold information about [business name]")

Dear [business name],

We are writing to tell you that we hold some information about your business, where it came from, and what you can do about it. The law requires us to tell you this. You do not need to do anything.

**Who we are.** Useful for Humans Ltd [company number, registered address]. We run a service called Stellar Local, which Tarkett funds for retailers in its network. Contact: privacy@stellarlocal.co.uk.

**What we hold, and where it came from.** In June 2026 we took a copy of Tarkett's public UK store locator (home.tarkett.co.uk/en_GB/store-locator). From it we hold your business name; the town and country you trade in; your address, postcode and map position; your website address; and, where Tarkett published one, a contact email address.

We then looked at your public Google Business Profile and recorded your Google rating, how many reviews and photos your listing has, whether it links to a website, and an identifier for the listing. From those we calculated a score and a band. The score is our own assessment; neither Google nor Tarkett publishes it.

**Why, and our lawful basis.** We hold this so we can provide the service Tarkett funds for retailers in its network, and measure how listings across the network are doing. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests. We have weighed those interests against yours, and recorded that assessment.

**Who sees it.** Our database provider (Supabase, in Ireland) and our hosting provider (Netlify) process it on our behalf. Tarkett is not given your score or anything else we worked out about your listing unless you choose to use Stellar Local. [If you join, Tarkett can see a summary of how your listing is doing: see our privacy policy.] We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it.** For as long as you are listed as a stockist on Tarkett's store locator and we provide the service to that network. We review this by hand, so a record may persist for a while after you stop being listed. [If the retention job is scheduled, replace with the actual period.]

**Your rights.** You can object to us holding this information, and we will delete it. You do not need to give a reason, and you do not need an account. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be corrected or deleted. Reply to this email or write to privacy@stellarlocal.co.uk.

We do not make automated decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We would rather you raised it with us first, but you do not have to.

Yours faithfully,

[Name], Useful for Humans Ltd
