Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information Stellar Local collects, why, and what we do with it. Stellar Local is a service that manages a retailer's Google Business Profile on their behalf. It is operated by Useful for Humans Ltd ("we", "us"), the data controller, under the Stellar Local brand for the Tarkett retailer network.

Who we are

  • Controller: Useful for Humans Ltd (trading as Stellar Local).
  • ICO registration: ZC200994.
  • Contact: privacy@stellarlocal.co.uk.

What the service does

With a retailer's permission, Stellar Local connects to their Google Business Profile and helps keep it active: publishing posts, replying to reviews, adding profile information, and reading back performance figures. The retailer connects their Google account using Google's OAuth sign-in and grants the Google Business Profile management scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage). That permission lets us act on the listings the retailer manages. The retailer stays the owner and can remove our access at any time from their Google account.

Review requests

A retailer can ask us to invite its customers to review it on Google. The retailer supplies each customer's email address and, if it wishes, their first name. We then send each customer one email on the retailer's behalf. It comes from "the retailer's name via Stellar Local", and a reply goes straight to the retailer. Everyone on the retailer's list gets the same email: an honest review is asked for, whatever the customer's experience was. We never offer anything in return for a review, and we do not choose whom to ask by how happy they seem. We send one request only, with no reminders.

Who is responsible. The customer list belongs to the retailer, which is the controller of it and decides whom to ask. We act as the retailer's processor: we use the details only to send the request the retailer asked for, and for nothing else. We do not pass customers' details to Tarkett, use them to advertise, or sell them. Keeping the "do not email" list described below is our own responsibility: we keep it so that a customer who has said no is never asked again by any retailer that uses Stellar Local.

What we keep, and for how long. We do not keep customers' email addresses or names, and we do not keep the retailer's list. They are used only while the emails are being sent. What we keep instead is a one-way code made from each email address, which cannot be turned back into the address:

  • a record that a request was sent, for 30 days;
  • a record that this retailer has already asked this address, for 12 months, so that the same retailer cannot ask the same customer twice;
  • the "do not email" list, for as long as the service runs, so that an unsubscribe is honoured for good.

How to stop them. Every request has an unsubscribe link. One click adds the address to our "do not email" list, and no retailer that uses Stellar Local will send it a review request again. You can also reply to the email, or write to privacy@stellarlocal.co.uk, and we will add it for you.

Legal basis

For retailer account data and acting on the Google profile, our legal basis is the performance of our contract with the retailer (and Tarkett as the funder of the service). For review requests, we act on the retailer's instructions as its processor; the retailer is responsible for having a lawful basis to contact its customers. We keep the "do not email" list because we must respect a person's objection to being contacted. For service and security communications, our basis is our legitimate interest in running the service safely.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Stellar Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data solely to provide and improve the Business Profile management service the retailer has asked for. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not allow humans to read it except where needed to run the service, with the retailer's permission, or as required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep account and profile data for as long as the retailer uses the service. If a retailer disconnects or asks us to close their account, we remove our Google access and delete their account data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records to meet a legal obligation. For review requests, see "Review requests" above.

Your rights

Retailers, customers who have been sent a review request, and anyone else whose personal data we hold, have the right to access their data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email privacy@stellarlocal.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we would like the chance to help first.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for material changes, let retailers know by email.
